# Soilless Growing Medium for Raised Beds

Shallow raised beds and planting boxes often skip native garden soil entirely and use a **soilless growing medium**, which avoids inherited weed seeds, compaction, and unknown fertility.

A widely used formula blends **equal parts by volume** of three components:

1. **Peat moss or coconut coir** — holds moisture and keeps the mix light.
2. **Coarse vermiculite** — adds aeration while also retaining water.
3. **Blended compost** — ideally from several different sources, to supply a broad range of nutrients and soil biology.

A depth of about **6 inches (15 cm)** of this medium is enough to grow most herbs and vegetables: the mix stays loose and friable and lets roots spread easily, which in turn supports closer plant spacing than heavy garden soil allows. Because the compost fraction is gradually consumed, a handful of fresh compost is worked in between crops rather than replacing the whole bed.
